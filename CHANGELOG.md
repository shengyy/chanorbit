# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Structure engine: inclusion, fractals, strokes with extension, same-time revision, canonical stroke centres, MACD(12, 26, 9) interval power, candidate stroke and the geometry snapshot.
- `chanorbit geometry` command: one JSON object of columnar bars (`dt` as `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS`, positive prices up to 1e9, volume up to 1e15, at most 200,000 bars) with non-decreasing `dt` (adjacent equal times are revisions, a decrease is an error), per-line errors including non-UTF-8 lines, single request or JSON Lines batch on stdin, deterministic output order; one geometry snapshot per request with each fact once; schema `chanorbit.geometry/1`.
- Regression baselines in `tests/golden/` and an end-to-end stdin→stdout test.
- `pyproject.toml`: the executable as a maturin `bin` wheel, installable with `uv pip install` or as a git dependency.
- `chanorbit stages` command (schema `chanorbit.stages/1`): replays the request one input at a time and answers with the confirmed buy-side second, third and second-like stages of the last input — centre, role strokes, first-known time, age in observed inputs, whether the move after the retest has passed the retest's start, optional `observe_from` (a string; `null` is refused) starts observation later, and `geometry` refuses the field, `null` included. A time revised within one request is observed once, after its last version. `--version` lists both schemas.
- `chanorbit levels` command (schema `chanorbit.levels/1`): one instrument, several period series coarsest first, bars labelled by the end of their period. Per level: a stroke window, raw and DIF extremes, the unfinished segment and the path after its extreme, up to five recent centres with last-close position and state (`departed`, `inside`, `formed_out`) read from the raw path after the third member — an ended centre's state is the fixed record of how it was left, with its retest's role in the next centre — and same-direction comparisons. Each finer level is placed in the next coarser one: child times of the parent's points (last stroke, unfinished segment, extension, path after the extreme), whether the child has passed them, child forces and zones; a child series that starts inside the parent's last stroke is still zoned and says so. `--version` lists the third schema.
- Levels regression baselines in `tests/golden/levels/`.
- Stage regression baselines in `tests/golden/stages/`: each kind, after-retest states, no stage, first-known and observation-start boundaries, first time unknown at the observation start for each kind, revisions, more second-like series, retest low exactly at and one tick above the guard.
- `levels` (version 0.1.3): a `departed` centre state carries `new_extreme` — when the departure runs toward its exit side, whether its raw extreme is strictly beyond the lowest low (`zd`) or highest high (`zg`) of every other member; for a non-member that is `dd`/`gg`, an equal price is `false`; `null` against the exit side. Only a field is added: schema `chanorbit.levels/1` and every other value unchanged; levels baselines regenerated for the field alone.
- `tests/invariants.rs` (version 0.1.6): standing invariants over every baseline input series and the synthetic zigzag — MACD of every suffix converges to the full series within the seed's remaining weight, an extending last stroke reads as it completes, every power is recomputed from the bars and the reported MACD, and appending keeps earlier strokes, centres and indices. It replaces the zigzag-only append test in `tests/units.rs`.
- `chanorbit signals` command (version 0.2.0, schema `chanorbit.signals/1`): one instrument through 2 or 3 period series coarsest first (parent, middle, optional leaf). `completion(level, span, dir)` runs the canonical centre construction on the strokes starting inside a span (its first stroke entry only) and holds when the element with the span's extreme leaves the last local centre to a new extreme on a smaller same-direction MACD area than the centre's entry, confirmed by the opposite fractal at that extreme. `buy` is the third-buy nest: the parent's latest centre departed upward by a formed up stroke, a first retest whose finest-level low stays above `zg` and has not been followed by the departure's high (`armed`), completing on the middle level at that low (`ripe`, `turned` once its bottom fractal confirms) and on the leaf level, confirmed (`triggered`), keyed by centre start, departure end and low time. `sell` is the up-end nest: the middle level's latest up segment completing on the leaf, confirmed at its high, with `mid_divergent`, `mid_lower_high` and `parent_complete`; `mid_support` is the middle level's latest counted down stroke low. `observe_from` replays along the finest level, each level fed only bars labelled up to the step, and lists the first step of each `(side, key, stage)` with the object; a step equals the stateless call on the series truncated there. Buy stage `primed` (between `armed` and `ripe`): the middle level's latest element leaves the retest's local centre downward before B holds; `mid` is output whenever the local centre exists, as the completion or the move in progress (`last`, `new_extreme`, current `area_ratio`, `confirmed_at`). `sell` stands `turned` when the middle level's top fractal at `U`'s high is confirmed (also with 2 levels) and `triggered` on the confirmed leaf completion, same key, and carries `parent_vs_departure` {`new_high`, `area_ratio`}: the parent's latest up segment against the departure of the latest parent centre left upward. `triggered` needs `turned` (the middle level's bottom fractal confirmed at the low) and C, which is now a veto over the leaf down move from the start of the middle level's last segment to the low: the leaf completes there at the low (confirmed or not) or no qualified leaf centre lies inside it (`leaf_centre`, new on `buy`, `null` when C is not evaluated); nothing after the low enters C, and C waits until the leaf stroke holding the low is counted. A5: the retest low must not lie inside the core of an earlier parent centre. `buy` is `null` only when A1–A3 fail; a failing A4 (the departure's high reached after the low) or A5 caps the nest at `armed` with `mid`, `leaf` and `leaf_centre` `null`, and a new low (a new key) heals it. `--version` lists the fourth schema. `geometry`, `stages` and `levels` are unchanged (the centre state moved to `levels_state` and the levels reading of extension, counted strokes and tail elements is shared, without changing output).
- `tests/signals.rs` and `tests/signals_sell.rs` (series built in `tests/nest/`): synthetic 30m/5m/1m series built from one 1m pivot path for every buy stage, each condition against a series differing only in it (retest touching `zg`, a second retest, a high back at `H_dep`, no centre in the span, an equal low, an area not smaller, an unconfirmed fractal), `primed` against a latest element running up and against no local centre, the mirrored sell cases and attributes, sell `turned` before and after the 5m top confirms and against `triggered` with the leaf, both branches of `parent_vs_departure`, `ripe` while the 5m stroke extends with the 1m move confirmed against `triggered` one 5m bar later, a straight 1m drop triggering with no leaf centre, a rally after the low with 1m up centres leaving the stage and `leaf_centre` unchanged, a leaf centre found without an aligned first stroke, a reviewer's series whose 1m move into the low is still unfinished (`turned`, `leaf_centre` `null`), the veto never changing within a key over every truncation, a 1m centre left without divergence (`turned`), the low inside, at `zg` of and above earlier 30m cores (capped at `armed`), A4 and A5 healed by a lower low, the replay against stateless calls on every truncation, request errors and the binary's batch.

### Changed

- MACD availability (version 0.1.6, schemas `chanorbit.geometry/2`, `chanorbit.stages/3`, `chanorbit.levels/3`): a MACD value is reported only once it no longer depends on where the request starts. The EMAs are seeded at the request's first bars and the long EMA's seed keeps a weight of `(25/27)^(i − 25)` at bar `i`, so DIF, DEA and the histogram are now `null` for bars 0..144 and reported from bar 145, the first where that weight is below `1e-4` (derived in code from the parameters and the tolerance; before, they were reported from bar 33 while the contract said DIF exists from bar 26). Everything that reads MACD follows: `macd.bars` and segments, stroke and interval power (`warmup` now means a row within the first 145 bars), comparison areas and `between` values, child forces and stage divergence — a first point whose entry or departure area is unavailable is not established. A caller that needs MACD from a bar on sends at least 145 bars before it; the engine still drops nothing and adds no parameter. Baselines regenerated: geometry and levels change only in MACD-derived values and the schema string; stage baselines get 145 one-price bars at the first bar's high before each series (all but the 3- and 40-bar cases), which leave strokes, centres and every stage unchanged.
- `levels` child window: when a child series starts after the parent's last-stroke start (`child_starts_after` is `last_start` or later), the child window starts at its first stroke, so every child stroke inside the parent's last stroke is zoned; before, the window could start at the oldest listed centre and leave earlier child strokes out of the zones.
- `levels` extending last stroke (version 0.1.6, `chanorbit.levels/3`): an extending last stroke is that stroke, not yet ended, and is read to its current extreme — `end_at`, `end_price`, `high`/`low`, raw and DIF extremes and power over its start to the first bar holding the most extreme price after it, `newbar_count` to the NewBar after the one holding that price — with the new level field `last_stroke_extending: true`. It is finished as a stroke ending there would be (five unfinished NewBars from its end fractal's first NewBar), so while it extends it is normally not finished and not a centre member (`last_stroke_member` `not_finished`); centres, the window and the latest state follow. A departure in it is the stroke by its index, `member` equal to `last_stroke_member.member`; the third member of a centre, extending or not, is formation, so the centre is `formed_out` or `departed` after it, never departed through it. The in-progress reading equals the reading once a reverse move or a further fractal completes the stroke at the same extreme. Removed: `unfinished.kind` (`unfinished` is only the reverse segment, `null` while the last stroke extends), the `extension` and `last_extended` positions, the `extension_vs_last` row, the `extension` child point and the `tail` element; `last_vs_previous`, `departure_vs_entry` and `advance_vs_departure` read the extending stroke as above and are `in_progress` while it moves; the path after the extreme follows the last stroke's end as read when there is no unfinished segment (also when only one-price bars at its end price follow it, the one case the tail held); the child zone boundary `last_end` and the `last_stroke` point use the end as read, so the child strokes that make an extension lie inside the parent's last stroke. Geometry and stages are unchanged.
- Strokes (version 0.1.1, `chanorbit.stages/2`): a stroke's start is the most extreme raw price between its start fractal and its end fractal, so a low or high that the inclusion merge hid becomes the endpoint the previous stroke ends at; appending a stroke never carries the previous one across a price beyond its start (the last strokes are searched again from earlier); a last stroke whose end is exceeded is searched again from its start and replaced by the further end, or stays as it is (`kind = extension`) — it is no longer withdrawn; when a raw bar after its end has already passed its start, the last two strokes are searched again as one, so the earlier stroke ends at the true extreme. When moving a stroke's end to the next start would put a raw price beyond its own start inside it (an end bar that also passed the start), it starts again at that price and the stroke before follows. `geometry` and `levels` fields keep their meaning (schemas unchanged), and their values follow the new strokes; stroke `power` is taken over `(start_at, end_at]` of the new endpoints, which need not be fractal times. `stages` drops `withdrawn_rebound`, which the new strokes can no longer produce, and adds `retest_broken`: a retained raw low after the retest's end below the retest's low (a retest still extending past its low, which the old strokes withdrew). Baselines regenerated; the stage baseline `rebound_withdrawn` and the two withdrawn-rebound cases are removed, and one transformed levels case is cut earlier to keep a departure in the extension.
- Stroke endpoints (version 0.1.2): both ends of every stroke are taken from the retained raw bars, not from the fractals or NewBar boundaries. The start is the most extreme raw price after the previous stroke's start and before the end fractal; the end is the most extreme raw price after the start up to the end fractal, so a high or low that the inclusion merge hid in the end fractal's first NewBar or in the one before it is now the end, and the next stroke starts there. Extension is decided by raw prices after the end. A wide bar that reaches beyond both the stroke's start and the next stroke's end cannot end a stroke. The candidate anchors at the last stroke's end and ends at the most extreme raw price after it; the element cap no longer affects it, and the reasons `anchor_price_source_missing`, `anchor_not_in_engine_tail`, `no_forward_price_source` and `missing_coverage` are gone. Schemas and fields unchanged; a stroke's `raw_extreme` in `levels` now equals its end. Baselines regenerated; the levels `tail` baseline is replaced by a hand-built case for a departure against its exit side, and one synthetic 30m high is lowered two ticks.
- Strokes (version 0.1.4): a stroke forms only when at least three raw bars lie strictly between its two fractals' extreme bars, counted without inclusion (the second half of the 2007-09-18 new-stroke definition). When the most extreme opposite fractal fails it, no stroke forms and the window waits: a less extreme fractal is never taken, and a later fractal at the same price does not end it. On equal prices the first raw bar reaching the price holds it everywhere — stroke starts and ends, the extension and candidate end, levels' raw extremes — so a stroke end no longer moves to a later bar at the same price. Power keeps `(start_at, end_at]`; the contract now says why (each bar counted once along the sequence). Schemas and fields unchanged. Baselines regenerated: strokes whose fractals had fewer than three raw bars between them are gone (9 of 34 in `daily_limit_gaps`, 3 of 27 in `daily_walk`, 47 of 50 in `daily_min_len4`, where four NewBars leave the gap to decide), and endpoint times move to the first bar at the same price, with power following the new endpoints. Stage baselines whose stage no longer stands are rebuilt from new synthetic series (second, its guard pair and the rule tests' base; second-like within, `beyond_start`, first input, observation start and revisions; first time unknown for second and third), and two pairs that stood no stage since 0.1.3 (second `observe_from`, a second-like `beyond_start`) are rebuilt with them.
- Whole window (version 0.1.5): every command computes over every bar of its request; the caller owns the window. The engine no longer keeps at most 50 strokes nor drops the raw bars before the first kept stroke, so appending bars never re-segments the oldest centres from a cut, never shifts stroke indices and never re-seeds MACD at a moved head; MACD starts at the request's first bar. Schemas and fields unchanged (`strokes` lists every stroke, indices count from the request's first stroke). Baselines regenerated: every series' MACD and power follow the earlier MACD start (raw bars before the first stroke are now kept), strokes and centres are unchanged where a series had at most 50 strokes; `f5_session_1920` now holds 110 strokes (its last 50 as before) and its oldest centre starts at its true first member instead of the cut, and two 5m levels cases list their oldest centre from its true start (18 and 17 members instead of 14 and 11), with window positions following.
- `levels` (version 0.1.5, `chanorbit.levels/2`): when the latest centre's exit lies in the extension of the last stroke, or in that stroke while it extends, and that stroke comes after the centre's third member, the departure is the last stroke taken whole — from its start to the extension's current extreme, position `"last_extended"`, in progress — instead of the `"extension"` fragment after its end or the stroke cut at its old end. `member` and `new_extreme` follow that whole stroke, so the in-progress `departure_vs_entry` row already reads what the completed stroke will read. An extending last stroke as the advance of `advance_vs_departure` is read the same way; `last_vs_previous` and `extension_vs_last` are unchanged. When the extending last stroke is the third member, the departure stays `"extension"` (not a member) as in 0.1.4: the third member belongs to the centre's formation, and once a reverse move ends it outside the core the centre is `formed_out`, so the in-progress reading equals the completed one only for `"last_extended"`. The new departure value changes the meaning of `departure`, hence the schema. Levels baselines regenerated for this alone (and the schema string): three synthetic cases (two 30m, one daily) whose exit lay in the extending last stroke and one transformed 5m case whose exit lay in the extension now depart `last_extended` (the transformed one becomes a member, the 30m and daily ones take the extension's extreme), their departure rows move to `in_progress` with whole-stroke values; in another transformed 5m case the advance of `advance_vs_departure` becomes `last_extended`.
- Input contract: a bar with `high < low`, or with `close` outside `[low, high]`, is an `invalid_bar` error naming the field and index (equal ends stay valid).
- `rust-toolchain.toml` no longer lists clippy and rustfmt, so building the binary from source fetches only the minimal toolchain; the gates need `rustup component add clippy rustfmt`.

### Fixed

- A panic while computing one request becomes that request's `internal_error` (exit code 1) for every command, single and batch; the other lines are answered as before, in order. The release profile states `panic = "unwind"`.
- A stdout that can no longer be written, including a reader that closed the pipe, exits with code 3 without a panic, also for `--version` and `--help`; a closed stderr is ignored.
